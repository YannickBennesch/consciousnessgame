stages:
  - "Stage 1: Choose one space: a room, a wardrobe, a storage corner. Take out everything in it."
  - "Stage 2: Put back only what is used or loved. Give away, sell or recycle the rest. Throwing away is the last option, not the first."
  - "Stage 3: Give one thing of value to a person, without being asked and without expecting a return."
  - "Stage 4: Ask one person for something you need, and receive it without diminishing it or paying it back immediately."
completeWhen: all four stages are done. Players often find stage 4 harder than stage 2. The log entry names which was harder and why.
