Start or join a compost: in a garden, on a balcony, in a community garden. Bring kitchen scraps to it regularly and observe what happens to them week by week. Alongside, have one conversation with an older person about an ending in their life: a loss, a move, a farewell, a change of body. Listen more than you speak.
