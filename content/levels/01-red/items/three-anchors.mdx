topics: [stability-rhythm]
form: quest
duration: 4 weeks
stages:
  - "Stage 1 (week 1): Observe without changing anything. Each day, record when you wake, eat and go to sleep."
  - "Stage 2 (weeks 2 and 3): Choose three daily anchors, for example a waking time, a shared or quiet meal, and a time when screens are put away. Keep them on at least five days of each week."
  - "Stage 3 (week 4): Test flexibility. On one day deliberately break all three anchors, and notice what happens to the body, the mood and the next day. Return to the anchors."
completeWhen: the four weeks are done and the log entry describes which anchor supports the player and which, if any, has become rigid.
