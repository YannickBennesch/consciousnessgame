Create a single page that shows the player's material situation as it is: monthly income, fixed costs, debts, savings, and every open piece of paperwork (unpaid bills, unanswered letters, overdue applications). Then write down the fear that this page brings up, in one sentence, and next to it one concrete step that would reduce the cause of that fear.
