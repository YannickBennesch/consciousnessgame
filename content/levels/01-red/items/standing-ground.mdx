slug: standing-ground
title: Standing Ground
level: 1
topics: [trust-ground]
form: exercise
duration: 5 to 10 minutes
pairVariant: Partners stand back to back and let each other's weight be held, then step apart and stand alone.
kind: core
sensitive: false
---
Stand barefoot on earth, grass, sand or stone. Let the weight sink fully into the feet. Notice where the body holds itself up instead of letting the ground hold it: shoulders, jaw, belly. Let go of one holding with each out-breath. End by saying, silently or aloud: "I am carried."
