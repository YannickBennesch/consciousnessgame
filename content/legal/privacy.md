> **TODO (O-5):** Final text to be supplied by the authors. The points below describe what the software does and are a starting point, not legal advice.

- To start playing we store only your e-mail address and your player code.
- Everything else (birth date, birth time, place of birth, region) is optional. Birth details are encrypted in the database and are visible only to you.
- Your log entries, quests and gratitude notes are private by default. Staff never read log entries.
- There is no analytics inside the player profile and no tracking cookies. The only cookie is the sign-in session cookie.
- You can export all your data and delete your account at any time from Settings.
- Payments are handled by Stripe. No card data reaches this platform.
